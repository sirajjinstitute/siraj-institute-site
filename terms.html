1. Our service

Suqya provides live, one-to-one online lessons in the Quran (recitation, Tajweed, memorisation and revision, Ijazah preparation, understanding and reflection), Islamic Studies and Arabic, for children and adults. Each learner has a learning path, homework and progress that the learner and their linked parent or guardian can follow in the Suqya portal.

2. Who can enrol, and accounts

  • An adult may enrol themselves. A child must be enrolled by a parent or legal guardian, who accepts these terms on the child’s behalf and is responsible for the account.
  • Please give us accurate details and tell us when they change.
  • Keep your login private. Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

3. The free trial

The trial lesson is free: there is no payment and no commitment. It lets the teacher assess the learner’s level and lets you experience the teaching before deciding anything.

4. Plans, prices and payment

  • Plans and prices are shown on our pricing section in US dollars. Any price shown in Egyptian pounds is an approximate guide only.
  • A plan gives the learner a balance of teaching time, kept in minutes. Each lesson taught uses its length from the balance.
  • Payment is arranged directly with the institute, by Visa card or bank transfer; we do not use an online payment gateway at present. A plan’s teaching time is added to the balance once its payment is received, and a renewal is a new payment for the next plan.
  • If we change our prices, the change does not affect time you have already paid for, and we will tell enrolled families at least 30 days before a new price applies to a renewal.

5. Cancelling and missed lessons

  • If you tell us at least 24 hours before a lesson that the learner cannot attend, the lesson’s time stays in your balance.
  • A lesson cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice uses its time from the balance, unless we accept it as an emergency.
  • The Family Guide, shared when you enrol, explains makeups, rescheduling and emergencies in full.
  • If the teacher or the institute cancels a lesson, its time is not taken from your balance, and we will agree a replacement time with you.

6. Refunds and freezing

If a circumstance stops a learner from continuing, we refund the unused teaching hours in cash, or freeze the balance instead if you would like to continue later. Hours already taught are not refunded.

Request a refund or a freeze through WhatsApp. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 7 days of the request, though when it reaches your card can vary by issuer.

Nothing in these terms takes away any right you have under the consumer law of the country where you live.

7. Conduct and safety

  • Lessons and messages must be respectful. We may pause or end a service where a learner, family member or teacher behaves in an abusive or unsafe way.
  • For young children, we encourage a parent or guardian to be nearby during lessons.
  • Messages in the portal’s chats are reviewed automatically by our AI tools to improve service quality and catch urgent requests, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Do not share another person’s private information, or recordings or lesson materials, without permission.

9. Ending the service

You can stop at any time by telling us through WhatsApp or email; unused teaching hours are handled as described in section 6. We may end or suspend the service if these terms are seriously broken, and we will explain why.
