# Suqya Institute — Brand Identity

Owner decision: 2026-10-10 (Africa/Cairo).

- Institute name: **Suqya Institute** / **سُقيا**.
- Arabic identity: **القرآن سقيا الروح**.
- English slogan: **Suqya Institute nourishes the soul with the Qur’an.**
- Beneath the institute name, use **Nourishes the soul with the Qur’an**.
- The owner approved replacing the complete About, Mission, and Vision copy
  with the proposal discussed in the session. It connects spiritual nourishment
  with individual Quran and Arabic teaching, ongoing support, and knowledge
  expressed in character and action, with sincerity to Allah.
- The English preposition is **with**, the natural wording for this meaning.
- Remove the former lamp explanation and light-based brand slogans from this
  section and its matching header/footer copy.

This decision does not change the domain, contact email, logo assets, pricing,
programmes, admissions flow, or LMS behaviour.
