Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Suqya Institute (“Suqya”, “we”, “us”) teaches the Quran, Arabic and Islamic Studies online, one to one. Many of our learners are children, so we collect only what we need to teach them well, and we explain here exactly what that is.

This policy covers our website (suqyainstitute.com), the free-trial application form, the Suqya learning portal and app (app.suqyainstitute.com), and our WhatsApp and email conversations with you.

1. Who we are

The data controller responsible for your information is Suqya Institute, Cairo, Egypt.

For any privacy question or request, email suqyainstitute@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you apply for a free trial

  • For each learner: name, age, gender, the programmes chosen, and any goal or previous learning you tell us about.
  • For the parent, guardian or adult applicant: name, contact email, WhatsApp number, country and time zone.
  • Your preferred lesson days and times.

When you learn with us

  • Account details: name, login email, role (student, guardian or teacher), and a phone number where you give one.
  • Learning records: lesson schedule, attendance, lesson reports written by the teacher, homework, Quran and curriculum progress, trial-lesson assessment, weekly stories, achievements and certificates.
  • Audio homework: recordings the learner makes of their recitation so the teacher can review them.
  • Messages and images sent in the portal’s chats, including earlier versions of edited messages.
  • Subscription and balance: the package chosen, teaching time remaining (kept in minutes) and payment records. Payments are arranged directly with the institute (Visa card or bank transfer); our platform does not store card numbers.
  • Preferences, such as lesson reminders and push notifications on your devices.

When you contact us

  • WhatsApp: the number you write from and the messages in the conversation, including with our WhatsApp assistant.
  • Email: your address and what you write.

When you visit the website

  • Usage information collected by Google Analytics, such as the pages viewed, the device and browser, and approximate location. See Cookies and analytics.
  • Security records: the platform logs important account and administrative actions (an audit log) to protect your account and our records.

Teacher applicants

If you apply to teach with us, we collect the details, recordings and images you submit in the application. Only our administrators can see them.

4. Automated (AI) review of messages

Messages in the portal’s chats are reviewed automatically by Suqya Institute’s AI tools to improve service quality and catch urgent requests. Every chat shows a short notice saying so.

  • The message text is processed by our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), on our behalf.
  • The results go only to the owner and our active administrators.
  • This review never changes who can read your conversations and never deletes or edits your messages. It does not make decisions about you on its own: any action is taken by a person.

Our WhatsApp assistant answers families who message us first. It may tell you where to find information in your own account, and only your own details are ever shared with you. A message it cannot handle is passed to a member of our team.

5. Children

Most of our learners are children. A parent or guardian applies for them, gives us their details and agrees to these terms on their behalf.

  • A parent or guardian account linked to a child can see that child’s learning progress, balance, next lesson, attendance and homework, and can message the institute and the child’s current teacher.
  • A child’s own chat with their teacher is separate from the family chats.
  • We collect only what teaching needs. We never ask a child for more information than that, and we never use it for advertising.
  • A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete their child’s information at any time (see Your rights).

For families in the United States: we collect personal information from a child under 13 only through the parent or guardian who applies for them. By sending the application, the parent or guardian consents to us collecting and using the child’s information as described here, and our team contacts them directly before the first lesson. A parent or guardian can withdraw this consent and ask us to delete the child’s information at any time.

6. Who can see your information

  • Teachers see the learners they currently teach, for as long as they teach them.
  • Coordinators and administrators see what they need to run admissions, schedules and support.
  • Supervisors review teaching quality and do not see a learner’s phone number or guardian contact.
  • Linked parents and guardians see their own children’s learning information.

Access is enforced on our servers and in our database, not only in the app’s screens.

8. Cookies and analytics

The portal uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. The public website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

You can stop Google Analytics from measuring your visits by blocking its cookies in your browser or with Google’s opt-out browser add-on. The Privacy Policy and Terms pages do not use Google Analytics.

10. Security

Access rules are enforced by our servers and database. Recordings and images are kept in private storage and are opened only through short-lived links for people allowed to see them. Portal messages are protected in transit but are not end-to-end encrypted: our authorised staff and the AI review described in section 4 can read them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information (or your child’s);
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • limit or object to how we use it, including the AI review of messages;
  • send it to you or another provider in a usable format;
  • withdraw any consent you gave, such as a parent’s consent for their child.

Email suqyainstitute@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and a parent or guardian may make a request for their child.

If you are in the UK and unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). Residents of US states with privacy laws have similar rights, which you can exercise the same way; we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell enrolled families. Questions: suqyainstitute@gmail.com.
