FAQ

Questions parents ask

What does the Islamic Studies programme cover?

The foundations of faith, worship, manners and Islamic knowledge. Your child learns to explain each idea in their own words and to apply it in daily life.

What curriculum do you follow?

A structured, levelled Islamic Studies curriculum in English, together with the Jannat Al-Quran Islamic Studies books, Parts 1 and 2, by Shaykha Dina Essam, which Suqya teaches under licence.

Are lessons in English?

Yes. Islamic Studies lessons are taught in English, one to one on Zoom.

What age can children start?

From 4 years old. Lessons are one to one, so the teacher sets the pace for each child, and adults are welcome too.

How do I see my child’s progress?

Progress is recorded lesson by lesson in the student and parent portal, so you can see where your child is at any time.

Can my child study Islamic Studies and Quran together?

Yes. You can choose more than one programme, for example Islamic Studies with Tajweed & Recitation or Memorization & Revision; every lesson stays one to one.

Can my daughter learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers teach girls, sisters and young children. Just let us know when we arrange the trial.
