About Suqya

The Qur’an nourishes the soul

القرآن سقيا الروح

Suqya means giving water. The Qur’an does for the heart what water does for the earth, so we teach it the way rain falls: steadily, patiently, one learner at a time.

Our mission

To make Qur’an and Arabic learning reachable for families worldwide, through private lessons, qualified teachers and ongoing support, so every learner progresses with confidence and grows to love learning.

Our vision

Children and adults with a lifelong connection to the Qur’an: reciting it with care, reflecting on its meanings and living by its guidance, guided by the Sunnah of His Prophet ﷺ, with sincerity to Allah.

Our approach

A suitable tutor, a clear learning path, steady follow-up and progress the family can see, ayah by ayah, in the Suqya portal.

Why families choose Suqya

Learning you can see

The commonest worry with online lessons is not knowing whether anything is being learned. Suqya was built to answer it, lesson by lesson.

Expert native tutors

Native Arabic speakers, including Al‑Azhar University graduates and certified Quran teachers, trained to teach learners who are new to Arabic.

Truly one to one

Every lesson is private and live, with patient, engaging methods that suit children with short attention spans, including ADHD.

A fixed weekly timetable

Your lessons sit at set times in your own time zone, with an assigned teacher and attendance recorded for every lesson.

An ayah-by-ayah Quran journey

The teacher logs every ayah your child memorizes, revises or has corrected, so the surah index fills as they learn.

Recited, graded homework

Your child hears the teacher’s example, records straight from the browser and gets a grade and a note back.

Parents always in the picture

Balance, next lesson, attendance, recordings, a weekly story and a monthly report. Messages stay inside the portal, where the institute can see them.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do the lessons take place?

Live on Zoom, and strictly one to one, so the teacher’s full attention is on you or your child. The portal shows each lesson in your time and opens the teacher’s room when it’s time to join.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. There is no payment and no commitment. The trial lets the teacher assess the learner’s level and lets you experience the teaching before deciding anything.

How do I follow my child’s progress?

Parents get their own login to the Suqya portal: the next lesson, the remaining balance, attendance and homework over the last 30 days, the Quran journey ayah by ayah, your child’s recordings, the teacher’s story of the week and a monthly learning report.

With a family discount, do we study in a group?

No. Family and friends discounts only lower the price. Every learner keeps a private timetable, their own plan and a dedicated teacher; every lesson stays one to one.

What if we miss a lesson?

Life happens. Tell us at least 24 hours before and the lesson’s time stays in your balance. The Family Guide, shared when you enrol, explains makeups and emergencies in full.

Can my daughter learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers teach girls, sisters and young children. Just let us know when we arrange the trial.

Who are the tutors?

Native Arabic speakers, including Al-Azhar University graduates and certified Quran teachers, trained to teach non-native speakers and to keep lessons patient and engaging for children, including children with ADHD.

Install the Suqya app

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow ↑). On newer iPhones, tap … first, then Share.
  2. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap “Add”.
  3. Open Suqya from the new icon and tap “Turn on” for notifications.

Apple does not let websites install apps with one tap; these steps take a few seconds.

Getting the app ready… the Install button appears here in a few seconds. Or install it yourself:

  1. Open this page in Chrome (if you are inside WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram, tap ⋮ and choose “Open in Chrome”).
  2. Tap the Chrome menu ⋮.
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

Already installed? Open Suqya from its icon on your home screen.
