Who teaches your child

Why families choose Suqya. Plays from YouTube when you press play.

  • Native Arabic speakers, including Al‑Azhar University graduates and certified Quran teachers.
  • Female teachers for girls, sisters and young children.
  • Patient with short attention spans. Tutors adapt each lesson to the child, with engaging methods that keep children with ADHD focused.
  • One timetable, one teacher. Your child’s lessons sit at fixed weekly times with an assigned teacher.

  • Your own time zone. The portal shows every lesson in your local time.

Six programmes, one path

Choose what you want to learn; you can pick more than one. The trial lesson shows where to begin.

Learn to recite the Quran correctly, with clear pronunciation and Tajweed rules: from the first letter to a full recitation of the Qur’an.

  1. 4–5 weeks

    Arabic alphabet

    Each letter from its correct articulation point, its shapes at the start, middle and end of a word, and the short and long vowels.

  2. 2 weeks

    Reading full words

    Joining letters into words smoothly, with sukoon and tanween.

  3. 2–3 weeks

    Reading full sentences

    From single words to compound sentences, ready to open the Mushaf.

  4. 1.5 months

    Reading the Mushaf with Tajweed

    Reading directly from the Mushaf alongside a practical Tajweed course on the essential rules.

  5. 2–2.5 months

    Juz Amma and basic Tajweed

    Completing Juz Amma while mastering madd, ghunnah, qalqalah and the rules of raa, so the student can read anywhere in the Qur’an.

  6. About 2–2.5 years

    A full recitation khatmah

    At three classes a week, starting with a quarter of a hizb per class and growing with the student’s fluency.

Memorize new passages and strengthen previous memorization through regular revision. Every ayah is logged in the student’s Quran journey.

  1. First

    A starting point

    The trial lesson and early classes record what the student already knows, so revision starts from the truth.

  2. 2.5–3 years

    Intensive track

    One to two new pages per class, with revision, for a full memorization of the Qur’an.

  3. 4–5 years

    Regular track

    Half a page to a page per class, with revision. Suited to children and busy schedules.

An advanced recitation pathway toward Ijazah, subject to assessment and qualification.

  1. First

    Assessment

    Entry depends on an assessment of the student’s recitation and memorization.

  2. Then

    Preparation

    Focused recitation with a qualified teacher, at the pace the assessment recommends.

Ijazah is preparation subject to assessment and qualification, never a guaranteed certificate.

Explore Quranic meanings and reflect on their guidance for everyday life. One course, two approaches: stories and simple visuals for children, deeper context and application for adults.

  1. 3–4 months

    The foundation and Juz Amma

    Surah Al-Fatihah and the whole of Juz Amma: word meanings and surah stories for children, the purposes of each surah for adults.

  2. 4.5–5.5 months

    Juz Tabarak and the Meccan gems

    Juz Tabarak with Al-Kahf, Ya-Sin, Yusuf, Luqman and Al-Hujurat: faith, character and the stories of the prophets.

  3. 12–15 months

    The long surahs

    Al-Baqarah to At-Tawbah: the major stories for children; legislation, family and the Seerah for adults.

  4. 12–14 months

    Completion

    Surah Yunus to Surah Al-Hadid, completing the tafsir of the whole Qur’an, with Suqya’s Certificate of Completing the Simplified Tafsir.

Learn the foundations of faith, worship, manners, and Islamic knowledge, in an easy, practical style.

  1. 1.5 months

    Essential aqidah

    Knowing Allah, the five pillars of Islam, the six pillars of faith and tawheed, through stories.

  2. 2 months

    Practical fiqh and worship

    Purification, wudu and prayer with its pillars and conditions, practised in class.

  3. 2 months

    The Seerah and the prophets

    The life of the Prophet ﷺ and the stories of the prophets, with their lessons.

  4. 1.5 months

    Manners and daily adhkar

    Kindness to parents, honesty and trust, and the adhkar of sleeping, eating, entering and leaving.

Build Arabic listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills step by step, from the first letter to confident speech.

  1. 4 weeks

    Alphabet and sounds

    Accurate pronunciation, including the heavier letters, and every written form of each letter.

  2. 4 weeks

    First words and greetings

    Numbers, colours, family and days; greeting and introducing yourself.

  3. 4 weeks

    Simple sentences

    Reading and building short sentences, with the basic pronouns.

  4. 2 months

    Everyday conversation

    Real situations: the market, the airport, school, food, hobbies and weather.

  5. 2.5 months

    Grammar and expression

    Nominal and verbal sentences, past and present, and short paragraphs without mistakes.

  6. 4–6 months

    Advanced fluency

    Stories and books, programmes in Arabic, confident speech, and a taste of Arabic rhetoric.

Questions, answered

How do the lessons take place?

Live on Zoom, and strictly one to one, so the teacher’s full attention is on you or your child. The portal shows each lesson in your time and opens the teacher’s room when it’s time to join.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. There is no payment and no commitment. The trial lets the teacher assess the learner’s level and lets you experience the teaching before deciding anything.

How do I follow my child’s progress?

Parents get their own login to the Suqya portal: the next lesson, the remaining balance, attendance and homework over the last 30 days, the Quran journey ayah by ayah, your child’s recordings, the teacher’s story of the week and a monthly learning report.

With a family discount, do we study in a group?

No. Family and friends discounts only lower the price. Every learner keeps a private timetable, their own plan and a dedicated teacher; every lesson stays one to one.

What if we miss a lesson?

Life happens. Tell us at least 24 hours before and the lesson’s time stays in your balance. The Family Guide, shared when you enrol, explains makeups and emergencies in full.

Can my daughter learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers teach girls, sisters and young children. Just let us know when we arrange the trial.

Who are the tutors?

Native Arabic speakers, including Al-Azhar University graduates and certified Quran teachers, trained to teach non-native speakers and to keep lessons patient and engaging for children, including children with ADHD.
