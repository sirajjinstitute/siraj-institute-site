Why a trial

What the free lesson gives you

You will get

  • A real, private lesson on Zoom with a qualified teacher
  • An assessment of the learner’s current level
  • The teacher’s reviewed summary, shared with you
  • A recommendation of how many lessons a week suit the learner

You won’t be asked for

  • Any payment for the trial
  • Any commitment to continue
  • A group class or a recorded demo: it is one to one and live
  • A decision on the spot: you choose a plan afterwards, if you wish

Before the lesson

How to make the most of the trial

  • Choose every programme you are curious about in the application; you can choose more than one.
  • Give the days and times that genuinely suit your week, and your time zone.
  • Tell us if you would prefer a female or male teacher.
  • Have a laptop, tablet or phone with Zoom ready, in a quiet place.
  • Stay nearby for younger children, so you can hear how the teacher works with them.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. There is no payment and no commitment. The trial lets the teacher assess the learner’s level and lets you experience the teaching before deciding anything.

What happens after the trial?

We share the teacher’s reviewed summary and how many lessons a week they recommend. If you continue, you choose a plan and weekly times, then get your timetable, your portal login and the Family Guide.

Which programmes can the trial cover?

Any of the six: Tajweed & Recitation, Memorization & Revision, Ijazah Preparation, Quran Understanding & Reflection, Islamic Studies and Arabic Language. You can choose more than one.

Can my daughter learn with a female teacher?

Yes. Qualified female teachers teach girls, sisters and young children. Just let us know when we arrange the trial.

How do the lessons take place?

Live on Zoom, and strictly one to one, so the teacher’s full attention is on you or your child. The portal shows each lesson in your time and opens the teacher’s room when it’s time to join.

What does it cost if we continue?

$14.99 per teaching hour; each lesson is 30 minutes. Three lessons a week is $89.94 a month for one learner, with family and longer-billing discounts applied automatically.
